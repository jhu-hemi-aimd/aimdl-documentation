# Detailed Emergency Procedures

For immediate contact information and the incident flowchart, see the [Emergency Quick Reference](emergency-quick-reference.md).

## Major medical accident

A major medical accident is any incident requiring more than first aid, including uncontrolled bleeding, unconsciousness, severe burns, suspected serious injury, or exposure to a significant hazard.

1. If it is safe, remove the person from exposure to the hazard. If you do not know why someone is unconscious or injured, do not approach until the hazard is understood.
2. Call JHU Security at **410-516-7777** and provide the location, nature of injury, hazards present, and your callback number.
3. Stay with the injured person if safe and administer first aid only if you are trained and comfortable doing so.
4. Send someone to meet emergency responders if available.
5. Notify AIMDL Staff as soon as immediate emergency response is underway.

## Minor medical accident

1. Administer first aid if trained. If not trained, call Security and request assistance.
2. During business hours, seek evaluation through the appropriate Homewood health service. Security can direct the user to the proper service. Occupational Health should be contacted for work-related injuries by employees, paid student workers, faculty, staff, and post-docs. Student Health may evaluate student researchers according to current JHU guidance.
3. Outside business hours, contact Security. Security can coordinate medical transport, custodial response, and documentation.
4. If assisting another person, escort them to treatment and provide details on the incident, including SDS information for chemical exposure.
5. Notify AIMDL staff as soon as practical.

## Fire or building fire alarm

!!! danger "Do not fight a fire"
    Do not fight a fire under any circumstances.

1. Alert nearby occupants and evacuate the lab using the posted exit routes in Figure 1 and on Stieff walls.
2. Close doors as you leave if doing so does not delay evacuation or increase risk.
3. Pull the nearest fire alarm if the alarm is not already sounding. Fire alarm locations are marked in Figure 1.
4. Evacuate to the rally point away from the building and remain available for accountability.
5. Call JHU Security at **410-516-7777** and provide the location and details of the fire or alarm condition.
6. Notify AIMDL staff after evacuation and emergency notification.

See [Lab Layout and Access](lab-layout.md) for the evacuation routes and rally point.

## Facilities incidents

Facilities emergencies include physical-plant conditions that create a safety issue or ongoing property damage, such as leaks, gas-line problems, loss of ventilation, electrical hazards, or building-system failures.

1. Notify AIMDL Staff immediately.
2. If staff are unavailable and the condition creates immediate safety risk or ongoing damage, call JHU Security.
3. For non-emergency facilities issues, consult AIMDL staff. If they are unavailable, contact the Homewood facilities issue line or Transwestern building contact.

## Hazardous material incidents

1. Notify AIMDL lab engineers for cleanup guidance and instructions.
2. Do not attempt cleanup unless all of the following are true:
    - The spill is small, less than approximately 500 mL.
    - You know what the material is.
    - It is not highly toxic, carcinogenic, reactive, or otherwise high hazard.
    - You have the correct spill-control supplies.
    - You have been trained.
3. If any condition is uncertain, if the spill is too dangerous, or if lab engineers are unavailable, call JHU Security.
4. Keep others away from the affected area until the hazard is controlled.

## Threatening or suspicious activity

Call JHU Security if you feel threatened or observe suspicious activity. From a campus phone, **911** or **6-7777** will dispatch JHU Security. From a wireless phone, call **410-516-7777**. The Security number is also listed on the back of the JHU ID card.
