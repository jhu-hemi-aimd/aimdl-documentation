# Safety

!!! warning "Authoritative safety documentation"
    Pages in this section are safety-critical: they require code-owner review
    and a 6-month review cycle. Pages marked `needs-review` are not yet approved
    for operational use. Follow current JHU policy, posted laboratory controls,
    required training, and instructions from AIMD-L staff.

This section applies to the CAIMEE AIMD Laboratory in Stieff G30, including the
G30A Control Room, G30 Primary Lab Space, G30C Preparation Space, G30D Machinery
Closet, and G30E Data Closet. It provides facility-wide safety information and
does not replace equipment-specific standard operating procedures (SOPs),
manufacturer instructions, or hands-on authorization.

## Lab Overview and Safety Philosophy

The AIMD-L in Stieff G30 supports high-throughput automation of materials
characterization and the integration of those data streams with artificial
intelligence models and decision-making. The mission requires multiple
experimental setups to operate in one shared space.

Because the facility co-locates lasers, X-ray instrumentation, robots,
conveyors, grinding and polishing equipment, compressed air, vacuum, chemicals,
hot surfaces, and sharp tools, hazards can overlap in ways that are not typical
of a single-instrument laboratory.

!!! warning "Safe science is good science"
    No work should begin until the user understands the hazards, required
    controls, required PPE, equipment status, and emergency response steps.
    When in doubt, pause the work and consult AIMD-L staff.
