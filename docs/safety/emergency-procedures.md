# Detailed Emergency Procedures

!!! danger "Call for help"
    For an emergency at the Homewood campus, call **JHU Public Safety at
    410-516-7777** or **6-7777** from a campus phone. Protect yourself and
    others before attempting to assist. Do not enter or remain in an area when
    the hazard is unknown or uncontrolled.

Use the [Emergency Quick Reference](emergency-quick-reference.md) for the
incident notification flowchart and contact list. This page provides the
corresponding response steps.

## Major Medical Accident

A major medical accident is any incident requiring more than first aid,
including uncontrolled bleeding, unconsciousness, severe burns, suspected
serious injury, or exposure to a significant hazard.

1. If it is safe, remove the person from continued exposure to the hazard. Do
   not approach an injured or unconscious person until you understand the
   hazard and can assist without placing yourself at risk.
2. Call **JHU Public Safety at 410-516-7777**. Provide the location, nature of
   the injury, hazards present, and your callback number.
3. Stay with the injured person if it is safe. Administer first aid only if you
   are trained and comfortable doing so. Do not move the person unless they
   remain in immediate danger and can be moved safely.
4. If another person is available, send them to the building entrance to meet
   and direct emergency responders.
5. Notify Matt Shaeffer or Joseph Nkansah-Mahaney as soon as the immediate
   emergency response is underway.

## Minor Medical Accident

1. Administer first aid if you are trained. If you are not trained or the
   person's condition is uncertain, call JHU Public Safety and request
   assistance.
2. Students should contact **Student Health and Well-Being Primary Care at
   410-516-3311** for non-emergency follow-up. Employees, faculty, staff,
   postdoctoral researchers, and paid student workers should contact
   **Occupational Health at 443-997-1700** for work-related injuries.
3. Outside business hours, contact JHU Public Safety. Public Safety can
   coordinate medical response and provide direction for follow-up.
4. If assisting another person, provide the treating service with relevant
   incident details, including the Safety Data Sheet for a chemical exposure.
5. Notify AIMD-L staff as soon as practical. All injuries and near misses must
   be reported according to current JHU and AIMD-L requirements.

## Fire or Building Fire Alarm

1. Do not fight a fire unless you are specifically trained and authorized to
   do so.
2. Alert nearby occupants and evacuate using the posted exit route.
3. Close doors as you leave if doing so does not delay evacuation or increase
   risk.
4. Pull the nearest fire alarm if the alarm is not already sounding. The
   nearest alarm pull station is next to the north exit door, as marked on the
   [lab layout diagram](lab-layout.md#lab-layout-and-emergency-exits).
5. Evacuate to the grass lawn in front of the Stieff Building, across the
   parking lot, and remain available for accountability.
6. Call **JHU Public Safety at 410-516-7777** and provide the location and
   details of the fire or alarm condition.
7. Notify AIMD-L staff after evacuation and emergency notification.

## Facilities Incident

Facilities emergencies include leaks, gas-line problems, loss of ventilation,
electrical hazards, or building-system failures that create a safety issue or
ongoing property damage.

1. Notify Matt Shaeffer or Joseph Nkansah-Mahaney immediately.
2. If AIMD-L staff are unavailable and the condition creates an immediate
   safety risk or ongoing damage, call JHU Public Safety.
3. For a non-emergency Homewood facilities issue, contact **Facilities at
   410-516-8063** or the building facilities contact listed on the
   [Emergency Quick Reference](emergency-quick-reference.md).

## Hazardous Material Incident

1. Notify AIMD-L staff for cleanup guidance and instructions.
2. Keep others away from the affected area until the hazard is controlled.
3. Do not attempt cleanup unless all of the following are true:

    - You know what the material is.
    - The spill is no more than **500 mL** and is within the limits of your
      training and the approved spill-response procedure.
    - The material is not highly toxic, carcinogenic, reactive, or otherwise
      high hazard.
    - You have the correct personal protective equipment and spill-control
      supplies.
    - You have been trained and authorized to perform the cleanup.

4. If any condition is uncertain, the spill is too dangerous, or AIMD-L staff
   are unavailable, call JHU Public Safety. During normal business hours,
   Health, Safety and Environment can be reached at **410-516-8798**.

## Threatening or Suspicious Activity

Move to safety and call JHU Public Safety if you feel threatened or observe
suspicious activity. From a campus phone, call **6-7777**. From a mobile or
off-campus phone, call **410-516-7777**. The Public Safety number is also
listed on the back of the JHU ID card.
