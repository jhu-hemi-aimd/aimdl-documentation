# General Safety

## Scope Authorization and Responsibilities

This document applies to the CAIMEE AIMD Laboratory (AIMD-L) in Stieff G30,
including the G30A Control Room, G30 Primary Lab Space, G30C Preparation Space,
G30D Machinery Closet, and G30E Data Closet.

The document identifies general hazards, required controls, emergency contacts
and procedures, and minimum expectations for training and conduct in the space.
It does not replace equipment-specific standard operating procedures (SOPs),
manufacturer instructions, or hands-on training.

### Authorized access

- Only persons who have completed the [Lab Access and Safety Training](lab-access-training.md), completed a space orientation, and been approved by the Lead Engineer may enter the lab space unaccompanied.
- Visitors, contractors, and untrained personnel must always be accompanied by an authorized lab user or AIMD-L staff member.
- The accompanying authorized person is responsible for ensuring that visitors and contractors follow all relevant precautions, including PPE requirements.
- Do not prop open access doors or otherwise allow untrained personnel into the laboratory.
- Only trained and authorized users may operate AIMD-L equipment or perform procedures covered by equipment-specific SOPs.

### Individual and shared responsibility

- Stop work if a task, setup, or condition appears unsafe.
- Ask for help if you are uncertain about a procedure, hazard, PPE requirement, or emergency response.
- Maintain housekeeping and labeling so that other researchers can understand what is present and what is in progress.
- Report incidents, near misses, damaged equipment, missing safety supplies, and unsafe conditions to AIMD-L staff.
- Do not bypass, defeat, or modify safety systems unless a written SOP and designated AIMD-L staff authorization explicitly allow it.

## Major Hazards at a Glance

| Hazard category | Where it may be encountered | Primary controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class 4 infrared laser radiation | FOBA laser, HELIX laser shock test area, OMTECH nanosecond laser, SPHINX nanoindenter laser heating | Enclosures, interlocks, written SOPs, laser training, rated eyewear, controlled access, beam-path control. |
| X-ray radiation | MAXIMA XRD/XRF system | Lead-steel interlocked enclosure, closed doors, dosimetry, trained users, manufacturer and AIMD-L procedures. |
| Robot and conveyor motion | Automated material handling system and instrument handoff areas | Stay clear during operation, use low speeds when programming, observe operator instructions, and use E-stops when needed. |
| Pinch, crush, and impact hazards | Equipment doors, stages, conveyance belts, robot work envelopes, pneumatic clamps and pistons | Keep hands clear, maintain clearance during motion, and verify the area is clear before closing doors or starting motion. |
| High voltage and energized equipment | X-ray power supplies, lasers, robotics, SPHINX nanoindenter, polisher, printers | Do not open panels or service energized components unless trained, authorized, and following lockout or de-energization procedures. |
| Compressed gas, compressed air, vacuum, chilled water | MAXIMA nitrogen cylinder, profilometer isolation table, polisher, machinery closet, lasers | Secure cylinders, use cylinder carts, protect hoses and lines, clean leaks promptly, and avoid trip hazards. |
| Chemicals and chemical waste | G30C Preparation Space, fume hood, chemical cabinets, satellite accumulation area | Label containers, use the fume hood, segregate incompatibles, use compatible gloves, and coordinate disposal through lab staff. |
| Hot surfaces and fire hazards | Lasers, hot plates, 3D printers, laser-processed parts | Keep combustibles away, use approved materials, monitor for combustion, and unplug hot plates after use. |
| Sharps and cutting tools | Sample preparation, hand tools, power tools, sharps disposal | Use cut-resistant gloves when appropriate, fixture workpieces, cut away from the body, and dispose of sharps properly. |

## General Rules and Practices

1. Wear lab-safe attire and required PPE before entering the primary lab space or connected laboratory spaces.
2. A lab coat and safety glasses are required for everyone entering the primary lab space and connected lab spaces.
3. Wear rated laser eyewear in the laser enclosure, when operating the OMTECH nanosecond laser, or whenever a Class 1 laser condition is not assured.
4. Do not bring food or drink into the laboratory.
5. Clearly label samples, chemicals, waste, and active experiments.
6. Keep work surfaces, walkways, equipment access areas, electrical connections, and emergency equipment clear.
7. Do not begin new work without considering safety implications, required controls, and applicable SOPs.
8. Do not disturb equipment, experiments, samples, or setups unless you are responsible for them or have permission.
9. Do not work alone when an SOP or hazard assessment requires a second person. Central automation and laser work require at least two people or other controls specified in the SOP.
10. Undergraduate researchers may not work alone; another person must be present in the lab space or the control room.
11. Report unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, missing PPE, and near misses to AIMD-L staff.
