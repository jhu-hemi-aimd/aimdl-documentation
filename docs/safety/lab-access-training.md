# Lab Access and Safety Training

Researchers must complete the required HEMI and AIMD-L safety training before
receiving unaccompanied access to the AIMD-L laboratory. Reading this page does
not by itself satisfy the training requirement.

!!! warning "Access requires approval"
    Lab access is granted only after the required training records have been
    reviewed and the researcher has completed an in-person laboratory
    walkthrough with Joseph Nkansah-Mahaney.

## Required Training Sequence

1. Review and follow the instructions in the
   [HEMI Lab Access Training guide](https://hemi.jhu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/HEMI-Lab-Access-Training-2025_10.17.25.pdf).
2. Complete the Tier 1 general safety training described in the guide:
    - Complete all six Department of Materials Science and Engineering safety modules in Canvas and save a screenshot of the completed grades.
    - Complete the required JHU myLearning courses and download the PDF completion certificate for each course:
        - Bloodborne Pathogens
        - JH | Fire, Hazard Communication and Construction Safety 2024
        - Chemical Waste Management
    - Complete *The Safe Use of Lasers in the Laboratory* in myLearning. Researchers who will actively use lasers must also complete *Becoming an Authorized Laser Operator* and identify **Matt Shaeffer as the LIS** when registering as a laser user.
    - Register for an iLab account when required for instrument reservations and billing.
3. Complete Tier 2 by reviewing the AIMD-L laboratory-specific safety documentation and completing any acknowledgment or additional training assigned by AIMD-L staff.
4. Submit the required screenshots, certificates, and supporting documents to either **Matthew Shaeffer** or **Joseph Nkansah-Mahaney** for review.
5. After the training records have been reviewed, complete an in-person walkthrough of the AIMD-L laboratory with **Joseph Nkansah-Mahaney**.
6. Wait for confirmation that laboratory access has been approved before entering the laboratory unaccompanied.

## Equipment Authorization

Laboratory access does not authorize a researcher to operate AIMD-L equipment.
Tier 3 equipment-specific training must be completed in person before using new
equipment. Follow the applicable instrument manual, equipment-specific SOP,
and authorization process.

## Training Records to Submit

Submit the records required by the HEMI guide and any additional records
requested by AIMD-L staff. At minimum, the HEMI guide identifies:

- A screenshot showing completion of the six Canvas safety modules.
- PDF completion certificates for the required myLearning courses.
- Laser-training completion records applicable to the researcher's work.
- Any additional registration or training documentation requested for the intended laboratory activities.

The linked HEMI guide is the authoritative source for the current institutional
training instructions. Confirm any questions or unusual access circumstances
with AIMD-L staff before beginning work.
