# Safety Data Sheet index

Find product-specific Safety Data Sheets (SDSs) for AIMD-L chemicals here.
Use the site search or your browser's Find function to locate a product or code.
Read the full PDF and check its product name, manufacturer, concentration, and
form against the container before use. Follow [Chemical handling and waste](chemical-safety.md)
and the applicable SOP.

## Access and maintenance

Backup SDS copies are kept in a **labeled file drawer in the G30A control room**.
Use these copies when the online collection is unavailable, including during a
network or power outage.

For each addition or replacement, the page owner and lab safety reviewers should
check the container identifier, product form and concentration, supplier, document
date, and PDF link. Keep the manufacturer's document intact and update this index
along with the PDF. Resolve outstanding entries before representing the collection
as complete. Review and approval follow the [documentation review policy](../policies/documentation-review.md).
