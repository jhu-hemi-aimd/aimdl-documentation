## About This Safety Guide

This safety guide applies to the CAIMEE AIMD Laboratory in Stieff G30, including:

- G30A Control Room
- G30 Primary Lab Space
- G30C Preparation Space
- G30D Machinery Closet
- G30E Data Closet

It identifies general hazards, required controls, emergency contacts and procedures, and minimum expectations for training and conduct.

!!! info "Equipment-specific procedures"
    This safety guide does not replace equipment-specific standard operating procedures, manufacturer instructions, or hands-on training.
