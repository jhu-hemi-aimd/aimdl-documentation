# Class 4 Laser Safety

!!! danger "Class 4 infrared laser hazard"
    AIMDL uses Class 4 infrared lasers. Class 4 lasers can burn skin and cause severe, permanent eye damage from direct, reflected, diffuse, or indirect beam viewing. At AIMDL laser wavelengths, invisible infrared light can be focused by the eye onto the retina and cause retinal injury.

- All laser operators must complete required laser safety training and equipment-specific training.
- Users must follow the relevant SOP, manufacturer manual, engineering controls, PPE requirements, access controls, and posted conditions.
- Laser eyewear must be selected for the wavelength and optical density required by the SOP or laser safety assessment.
- Reflective jewelry, watches, badges, tools, and accessories must be removed or controlled when they could enter a beam path.
- Keep eyes and face out of the laser plane and never intentionally view a beam path.
- The laser shock test area has dedicated safety documentation and SOPs that must be reviewed by laser users and regular AIMDL users whose work brings them near the enclosure.

!!! info "Equipment procedures still apply"
    This page provides global laser-safety requirements. It does not replace equipment-specific SOPs, manufacturer instructions, required training, or the dedicated laser shock safety documentation.
