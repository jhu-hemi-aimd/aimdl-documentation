## Table of hazards and mitigating measures

| Nature of hazard | Mitigating measures |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy equipment, trip hazards, and benches | Move deliberately. Watch your step. Keep workspaces clear while using equipment. |
| Pinch points from equipment doors | Wear required PPE. Before closing doors, confirm hands, tools, and samples are clear. |
| Sharp corners at shelves, tables, and instruments | Wear required PPE. Move deliberately and mind marked hazards. |
| High voltage on power supplies for X-ray, robotics, indenter, lasers, polisher, and printers | Recognize power-supply locations. Avoid stepping on or pinching power connections. Keep power areas clear and dry. Keep combustibles away. Do not open or work on power supplies unless trained, authorized, and working under de-energized or approved procedures. |
| Surge protectors and power cables | Keep surge units off the floor where practical. Keep surrounding areas clear and dry. |
| Handling solvents and other chemicals | Wear required PPE. Use the smallest needed volume in a fume hood or ventilated area. Keep away from heat and ignition sources. Use secondary containment. Label, store, and dispose through approved waste procedures. |
| Laser-generated particles from ablation, cutting, or engraving | Use required ventilation. Wear required PPE and respiratory protection when specified by SOP. Clean affected surfaces routinely to minimize debris buildup. |
| Infrared laser radiation | Complete laser safety and instrument-specific training. Follow all SOPs. Use enclosures and interlocks as required. Wear rated eyewear. Remove reflective accessories. Keep eyes and face out of the beam plane. Work with a buddy when required. |
| Sharp cutting tools | Wear required PPE. Use cut-resistant gloves when appropriate, except around rotating equipment. Fixture workpieces. Cut away from the body. Store tools safely and dispose of sharps in approved containers. |
| X-ray radiation | Operate only with doors closed and interlocks satisfied. Maintain dosimetry as required and inspect quarterly according to the AIMDL program. |
| Robot conveyance pinch points | Wear required PPE. Stay clear of conveyance, motors, and actuators during operation. Follow operator instructions. |
| Robot arm motion | Wear required PPE. Stay clear during operation. When programming, operate at the lowest practical speed and maintain clearance. |
| Hot plates and 3D printers | Wear required PPE. Keep flammables at least 24 inches from hot surfaces. Unplug hot plates after use. Keep 3D printer areas clear of unrelated items. |
