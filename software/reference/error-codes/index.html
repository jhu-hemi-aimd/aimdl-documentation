    Error codes¶

    #software #controls #errors

    Canonical definitions from apps.system.errors.ERRORS_BY_CODE in AIMDDS. This reference contains 170 definitions. Edit the AIMDDS definitions and regenerate this file to change the catalog.

    Severity values are GOOD=0, WARNING=1, ERROR=2, and CRITICAL=3. A code describes a fault; active/acknowledged state and diagnostic details belong to individual occurrences. Acknowledgment does not clear a fault.

    The registry reserves 5500–5999 for Zygo and 6000–6499 for the engraver; reserved ranges are not implemented controller definitions.

    System Allocated range Definitions
    HELIX 1000-1999 43
    MAXIMA 4000-4999 35
    SPHINX 5000-5499 19
    COORD 6500-6999 4
    Run Manager 10000-10999 69

    HELIX (1000-1999)¶

    Experiment Settings (1000-1049)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.experiment_settings.

    1010 — Invalid flyer stack configuration¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide the flyer stack as a valid configuration object containing the expected flyer metadata.

    1011 — Invalid flyer material¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide the flyer material as a string.

    1012 — Invalid flyer thickness¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide the flyer thickness as an integer.

    1020 — Invalid pulse points configuration¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide pulse points as a sequence of integer (row, column) pairs within the supported flyer grid.

    1021 — Duplicate pulse points specified¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide each flyer position only once. Duplicate positions are removed while preserving the first occurrence.

    1030 — Invalid waveplate angles configuration¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide waveplate angles as a sequence of finite numeric values.

    1031 — Waveplate angles are out of allowable bounds¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide waveplate angles within the supported range. Values outside the range are clamped to the nearest allowable bound.

    1040 — Invalid PDV probes configuration¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide PDV probe numbers as integers corresponding to configured PDV probes.

    1041 — Duplicate PDV probes specified¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Experiment settings

    Recommended action: Provide each PDV probe only once. Duplicate probes are removed while preserving the first occurrence.

    Heating Laser (1050-1099)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.heating_laser.

    1051 — Heating laser connection failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Heating laser

    Recommended action: Verify that the heating laser controller is powered on, connected to the control network, and reachable at its configured address.

    Motion Stage (1100-1149)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.motion_stage.

    1101 — Motion stage connection failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Motion stage

    Recommended action: Verify that the motion stage is powered on, connected to the control network, and reachable at its configured address.

    1110 — Motion stage motors failed to enable¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Motion stage

    Recommended action: Clear any motion stage faults, verify that the axes can be enabled, and confirm that all interlocks permit motion.

    1111 — Motion stage motors failed to disable¶

    Severity: CRITICAL (3)

    Source: HELIX: Motion stage

    Recommended action: Stop the motion stage manually and verify that all axes are disabled before approaching the equipment.

    1120 — Motion stage move request failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Motion stage

    Recommended action: Verify motion stage communication, clear controller faults, and confirm that the requested destination is permitted.

    1121 — Motion stage failed to reach its destination¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Motion stage

    Recommended action: Inspect the stage for an obstruction or axis fault and verify that the requested position can be reached safely.

    Oscilloscope (1150-1199)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.oscilloscope.

    1151 — Oscilloscope connection failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Oscilloscope

    Recommended action: Verify that the oscilloscope is powered on, connected to the control network, and reachable at its configured address.

    1160 — Oscilloscope acquisition failed to stop¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Oscilloscope

    Recommended action: Check oscilloscope communication and stop acquisition from the instrument if it remains active.

    1161 — Oscilloscope acquisition failed to start¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Oscilloscope

    Recommended action: Verify the selected channels and oscilloscope state, then clear any instrument or communication faults.

    1170 — Oscilloscope waveform save could not be confirmed¶

    Severity: WARNING (1)

    Source: HELIX: Oscilloscope

    Recommended action: Check the oscilloscope storage location and communication status. The laser may already have been triggered; do not repeat the shot without reviewing the experiment state.

    PDV (1200-1249)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.pdv.

    1201 — PDV connection failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: PDV

    Recommended action: Verify that the PDV is powered on, connected to the control network, and reachable at its configured address.

    1210 — PDV outputs failed to turn off¶

    Severity: CRITICAL (3)

    Source: HELIX: PDV

    Recommended action: Turn off the PDV outputs manually and verify that all configured laser outputs are disabled before servicing the system.

    1211 — PDV configuration failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: PDV

    Recommended action: Verify PDV communication and the configured source wavelengths, powers, slots, and channels.

    1212 — PDV outputs failed to turn on¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: PDV

    Recommended action: Verify PDV communication and clear any source or output faults before enabling the requested probes.

    1213 — PDV measurements could not be read¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: PDV

    Recommended action: Check PDV communication and confirm that every requested probe is configured and returning complete measurements.

    Power Meter (1250-1299)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.power_meter.

    1251 — Power meter connection failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Power meter

    Recommended action: Verify that the power meter is powered on and available at the configured serial port, then check its cable and port permissions.

    1260 — Power meter range change failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Power meter

    Recommended action: Check power meter communication and verify that the requested measurement range is supported and active.

    Pulse Generator (1300-1349)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.pulse_generator.

    1301 — Pulse generator connection failed¶

    Severity: ERROR (2)

    Source: HELIX: Pulse generator

    Recommended action: Verify that the pulse generator is powered on, connected to the control network, and reachable at its configured address.

    1310 — Pulse generator output failed to disable¶

    Severity: CRITICAL (3)

    Source: HELIX: Pulse generator

    Recommended action: Disable the pulse output manually and verify that the output is off before approaching or servicing the laser system.

    Top Camera (1350-1399)¶

    Source: apps.system.errors.helix.top_camera.

    1351 — Top camera connection failed¶
